# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 21 September 2026*

Classroom Timer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## Data collection

The app collects no user data. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers, and no accounts.

## Screen recording permission

Classroom Timer asks for screen recording permission so it can take a snapshot of your screen and use it as the placeholder background behind the timer.

The snapshot is taken on your machine, used only to draw that background, and never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, shared, or stored anywhere outside your Mac.

## Local data

All app data, including preferences and any images you choose, stays on your machine.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page.
